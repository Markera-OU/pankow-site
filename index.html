03 / HOW IT WORKS

Connect. Chase. Paid.

Connect once. Pankow reads your open invoices and starts with the ones that are already late.

01 / CONNECT

Connect QuickBooks or Xero

Pankow reads your open invoices and finds the ones already overdue, including invoices that went overdue before you connected.

02 / CHASE

Pankow chases each debtor

Each customer gets a sequence matched to their invoice age and how they have paid before. Gentle for a good payer who slipped once, firmer for a chronic late payer.

03 / PAID

You get paid, Pankow learns

When an invoice is paid, the sequence stops and Pankow records how that debtor paid. The next chase uses what worked.

04 / WHAT IT DOES

Built for the small firms the big tools ignore.

Micro-dunning, not enterprise collections.

Retroactive pickup

Pankow chases invoices already overdue when you connect. Native reminders only cover new invoices. Pankow covers the backlog too.

Per debtor sequences

Each debtor gets a sequence matched to their invoice age and payment history. Not one template for everyone.

Your firm’s voice

Reminders are drafted in your firm’s voice, learned from your past emails. They read like you wrote them.

Pay link on every reminder

Every reminder carries a pay link. A debtor can pay without replying to an email.

Payment behaviour history

Pankow remembers how each debtor has paid before, so sequences get smarter over time instead of starting from scratch.

Accountant white label

Accountants can run Pankow for their clients under their own firm’s identity, with one console for all clients.

06 / QUESTIONS

Asked, answered.

Does Pankow guarantee I will get paid?No. Pankow chases professionally and persistently, and makes it easy for debtors to pay. Payment is the debtor’s decision. We never promise payment.

Which accounting software does it work with?QuickBooks Online and Xero.

What about invoices that are already overdue?That is the point. Pankow picks them up on day one and starts chasing them. Native reminders only cover invoices that go overdue after setup.

Will reminders sound like my firm?Yes. Pankow learns your firm’s voice from your past emails and drafts reminders to match it.

Can my accountant run Pankow for me?Yes. Accountants can use the Accountant plan to run Pankow for their clients, with each client’s reminders sent under that client’s own identity.
