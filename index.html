How it works

Connect once. Pankow finds what is already overdue and starts chasing it.

1

Connect QuickBooks or Xero

Connect your accounting software in one click. Pankow reads your open invoices and finds the ones already overdue — including invoices that went overdue before you connected.

2

Pankow chases each debtor

For each customer and invoice age, Pankow sends a sequence of reminders in your firm’s voice — email, SMS, statement, and a pay link. The tone adapts to each debtor’s payment history.

3

You get paid, Pankow learns

When an invoice is paid, the sequence stops and Pankow records how that debtor paid. The next chase is better because Pankow knows what worked.

What Pankow does

Built for the small firms that the big dunning tools ignore.

Retroactive pickup

Pankow chases invoices that are already overdue when you connect. Native reminders only cover new invoices — Pankow covers the backlog too.

Per-customer sequences

Each debtor gets a sequence matched to their invoice age and payment history — gentle for a good payer who slipped once, firmer for a chronic late payer.

Your firm’s voice

Reminders are drafted in your firm’s voice, learned from your past emails. They read like you wrote them, not like a robot.

Pay link and late fees

Every reminder carries a pay link, and late fees can be added where your terms allow. Debtors can pay without replying to an email.

Payment-behaviour history

Pankow remembers how each debtor has paid before, so sequences get smarter over time instead of starting from scratch.

Accountant white-label

Accountants can run Pankow for their clients under their own firm’s identity, with one console for all clients.

Frequently asked questions

Does Pankow guarantee I will get paid?

No. Pankow chases professionally and persistently, and makes it easy for debtors to pay, but payment is the debtor’s decision. We never promise payment.

Which accounting software does Pankow work with?

QuickBooks Online and Xero.

What about invoices that are already overdue?

That is the point. Pankow picks up invoices already overdue when you connect and starts chasing them. Native reminders only cover invoices that go overdue after setup.

Will reminders sound like my firm?

Yes. Pankow learns your firm’s voice from your past emails and drafts reminders to match it. You can review the wording before anything is sent.

Can my accountant run Pankow for me?

Yes. Accountants can use the Accountant plan to run Pankow for their clients, with each client’s reminders sent under that client’s own identity.
