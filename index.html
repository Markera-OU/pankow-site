Questions, answered.

Does Pankow guarantee I will get paid?
No. Pankow chases professionally and persistently, and makes it easy for debtors to pay. Payment is the debtor's decision. We never promise payment.

Which accounting software does it work with?
QuickBooks Online and Xero.

What about invoices that are already overdue?
That is the point. Pankow picks them up on day one and starts chasing them. Native reminders only cover invoices that go overdue after setup.

Will reminders sound like my firm?
Yes. Pankow learns your firm's voice from your past emails and drafts reminders to match it.

Can my accountant run Pankow for me?
Yes. Accountants can use the Accountant plan to run Pankow for their clients, with each client's reminders sent under that client's own identity.
