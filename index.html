02 / WHAT IT DOES

Built for the small firms the big tools ignore.

Micro-dunning, not enterprise collections.

  1. 01

    Retroactive pickup

    Pankow chases invoices already overdue when you connect. Native reminders only cover new invoices.

  2. 02

    Per-debtor sequences

    Each debtor gets a sequence matched to their invoice age and payment history. Not one template for everyone.

  3. 03

    Your firm’s voice

    Reminders are drafted in your firm’s voice, learned from your past emails.

  4. 04

    Pay link on every reminder

    Every reminder carries a pay link. A debtor can pay without replying to an email.

  5. 05

    Payment behaviour history

    Pankow remembers how each debtor has paid before, so sequences get smarter over time.

  6. 06

    Accountant white label

    Accountants can run Pankow for their clients under their own firm’s identity, with one console for all clients.

03 / HOW IT WORKS

Connect. Chase. Paid.

06 / QUESTIONS

Asked, answered.

Does Pankow guarantee I will get paid?

No. Pankow chases professionally and persistently, and makes it easy for debtors to pay. Payment is the debtor’s decision. We never promise payment.

Which accounting software does it work with?

QuickBooks Online and Xero.

What about invoices that are already overdue?

That is the point. Pankow picks them up on day one and starts chasing them. Native reminders only cover invoices that go overdue after setup.

Will reminders sound like my firm?

Yes. Pankow learns your firm’s voice from your past emails and drafts reminders to match it.

Can my accountant run Pankow for me?

Yes. Accountants can use the Accountant plan to run Pankow for their clients, with each client’s reminders sent under that client’s own identity.
