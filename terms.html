The service

Pankow is a micro-dunning service for small firms. It connects to a firm's QuickBooks Online or Xero account, reads invoices that are already overdue, and sends payment reminders to the firm's debtors in the firm's voice, by email, until the invoice is marked paid. The firm controls which invoices are chased and can stop reminders at any time.

No guarantee of payment

Pankow does not guarantee payment. It sends reminders; it does not collect money, does not process payments, and does not move funds between any parties. Whether a debtor pays is entirely up to the debtor. Pankow is not a debt collection agency and does not act as one.
